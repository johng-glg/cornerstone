Read this before you book

This is a notarial service only. A notary verifies who you are and witnesses your signature. A notary does not read your document for accuracy, explain what it means, tell you whether to sign it, or prepare it for you.

No legal advice, no attorney client relationship. Booking a notarization does not make you a client of any law firm, and nothing here is legal advice. If you need legal help, arrange representation separately.

Our notaries are commissioned online notaries public and may serve signers located anywhere in the United States. Some documents and some receiving parties do not accept electronic notarization. Check with whoever is receiving your document before you book.

What we will not notarize. Vital records such as birth, death, or marriage certificates. Documents where you cannot present valid unexpired government issued photo identification. Any document where the signer does not appear to understand what they are signing or is not signing willingly.
